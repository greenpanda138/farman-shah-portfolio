About me

Hey, I'm Farman Shah.

I'm a full-stack developer and digital marketer who helps businesses win online — end to end. Most developers hand you a website and disappear. Most marketers can't touch code. I do both: I design, build, and then actually grow the thing.

My sweet spot is the intersection of engineering and revenue: Shopify stores with custom Liquid sections, e-commerce sites wired for conversion, and Meta ad campaigns managed with a developer's love of data.

When I'm not shipping, I'm studying what makes people click — consumer psychology, direct-response copy, and the occasional deep dive into animation and storytelling.
